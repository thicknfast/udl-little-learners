## Predicting Barriers

UDL starts before students walk through the door. This template walks you through anticipating where students might get stuck in a lesson or routine and designing supports in advance, so fewer kids need rescuing in the moment.

### How to Use

- Choose one lesson, center, or routine that isn't going as smoothly as you'd like
- Name the goal, then list the barriers students might hit
- Plan a design move for each barrier and try it
