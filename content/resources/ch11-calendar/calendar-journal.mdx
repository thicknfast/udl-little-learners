## Calendar Journal

Calendar time can have a participation problem. One student is called up to the board, and the others are watching — or not watching. This calendar journal puts a pencil in every hand.

### Two Versions

- **Scaffolded Version.** Students circle the day of the week, choose from weather icons, and fill in guided sections for date, patterns, and the Number of the Day.
- **Open-Ended Version.** The same structure with more open responses. Students write the day name, draw or describe the weather, and have more space to show what they know.

Same page, same routine, different entry points.

### How to Use

- Click **Open Resource** to make your own copy in Google Slides and customize it
- Print both versions and let students choose which one to use each day
- In the Number of the Day section, invite tally marks, ten frames, coins, fingers, dots, base-10 blocks, or their own way
