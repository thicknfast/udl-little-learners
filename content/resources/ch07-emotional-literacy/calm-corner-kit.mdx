## My Calm Corner Kit

Everything you need to set up or refresh a calm corner, with visuals so every child can name a feeling and choose a strategy on their own.

### What's Inside

- A feeling-identification poster
- Cut-apart feeling cards
- Calming strategy cards
- A step-by-step calming down worksheet
- A welcome sign
- Blank cards to add your own
