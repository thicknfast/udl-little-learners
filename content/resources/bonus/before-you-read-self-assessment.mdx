## Before You Read

Start here before Chapter 1. This self-assessment helps you take stock of what you already do, notice where UDL is already showing up in your classroom, and choose a focus for your reading.

### How to Use

- Complete it before you start the book, then revisit it when you finish
- Use it with a colleague or book study group to compare notes
- Pick one or two areas to focus on as you read
