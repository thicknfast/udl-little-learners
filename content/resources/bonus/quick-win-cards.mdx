## Quick Win Cards

Each card has one UDL strategy you can try tomorrow. No planning, no prep, just a small change that makes your classroom more accessible.

### How to Use

- Cut them apart and pull one a week
- Keep a stack by your planning space for a quick idea
- Use them as a team discussion starter at staff or PLC meetings
