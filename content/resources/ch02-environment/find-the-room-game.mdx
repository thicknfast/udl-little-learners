## Find the Room Game

Watch this quick video example of the "Find the Room" game in action. Shared by @where_learning_meets_play on Instagram, this reel shows a scavenger hunt activity where students search the classroom to find the tools and materials they'll use regularly.

### Why This Activity Works

- It gives students a hands-on introduction to where things live in the room
- It builds independence by helping students locate materials on their own
- It turns environment set-up into an engaging, low-stakes game early in the year
