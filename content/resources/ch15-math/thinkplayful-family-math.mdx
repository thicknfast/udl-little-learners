## Everyday Math with Families

We want our students to see math as something people use in real life, not just something that happens at school. ThinkPlayful helps families make that connection at home. It's a free app for families of children ages 3 to 6. Friendly animated guides talk parents through short, no-prep activities like sorting, counting, shapes, patterns, and measuring. Most of the play happens off-screen, around the house or outside.

### Why Share It with Families

- Gives families easy, playful ways to talk about math in everyday moments
- Activities are hands-on and work indoors or out, with no special materials
- Families can save photos of their activities in a journal and share them with you
- Built with funding from the National Science Foundation, and studied for its effect on parent-child connection and math anxiety

### How to Share It

- Add a link in your family newsletter or classroom app
- Mention it at back-to-school night or family conferences
- Invite families to share a photo of a math moment from home and celebrate it in class

### Good to Know

The app is free on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and is currently available in English only. It asks for an account and stores photos families add, so let families know what to expect when they sign up.
