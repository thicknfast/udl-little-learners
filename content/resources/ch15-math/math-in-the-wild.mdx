## Math in the Wild Example

This Instagram reel from **@mrallenmath** is a great example of Math in the Wild — noticing and naming math as it appears in everyday, real-world contexts.

Use it as a model for encouraging students to look for math outside the classroom: in patterns, shapes, quantities, and relationships they encounter in their daily lives.

### How to Use This in Your Classroom

- Show the reel as a discussion starter or warm-up
- Invite students to share their own "math in the wild" observations
- Create a class anchor chart or display where students can add examples they find
