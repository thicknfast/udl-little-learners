## Quantity Building Routine

This Instagram carousel from **@aubreeteaches** walks through a quantity building routine — a structured whole-group practice that helps young learners develop deep number sense by building and representing quantities in multiple ways.

### Why It Supports UDL

- Offers multiple means of representation (visual, physical, verbal)
- Every student can participate at their own level
- Repeated structure lowers cognitive load so students can focus on the math
